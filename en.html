Why not another fitness app

Distraction-free

Bilingual

The UI and all 157 exercise guides come in Chinese and English, following the system or switched manually.

How it differs from openGym

openGym (DuarteSantos8/openGym) is a project we respect on the same side: self-hosted, your data, clear per-muscle views — parts of LeanLift's analytics borrow its ideas. But the two put "data ownership" in different places, and the day-to-day picture differs completely.

One line: openGym is a training archive on your own server; LeanLift is a coach in your pocket — zero deployment, focus mid-set, and an AI that knows your data.

FAQ

Does it really work fully offline?

Yes. Training, logging, analytics, plans, the weekly recap and auto backups all run locally. The three networked extras (calendar, AI, update checks) are all optional.

Where is my data stored? How do I move it?

Local SQLite on your phone. A snapshot is saved after every workout (7 daily + 4 weekly), and Settings offers full JSON export/restore and CSV export — copy the archive to move devices.

Does the AI upload my training data?

Only when you actively use an AI feature, and only the needed content (e.g. an 8-week summary) goes directly to the provider you configured. Keys stay on-device; there is no middleman server. The weekly AI report is off by default and, once enabled, generated once in the foreground.

LeanLift or openGym?

Want zero deployment, focus mid-set, an overload engine and optional AI — LeanLift. Already running a NAS/server, prefer browser management, or migrating from FitNotes/Strong/Hevy — openGym is great too.

How do updates work?

Settings → App Update, one tap. Or download the latest APK from this page and overwrite-install — data survives.

What does it cost?

Nothing. MIT open source, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics.
